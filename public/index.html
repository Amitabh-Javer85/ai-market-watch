Sources & timing

Quotes and chart history come directly from TradingView. Feed venues and delays can differ by instrument. Refresh reloads the quote panels and checks for an updated peer comparison.

Peer comparisons use GetMarketHistory’s unadjusted daily bars, including extended hours. They can lag current quotes by about two days. Corporate actions can affect the reported moves. A group’s median, breadth and leader use companies with prices on both displayed dates.
